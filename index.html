Ski Lease Rooms

Each weekend, members register by Tuesday evening. Then an optimizer assigns the beds. It gives priority to people who got worse beds in earlier weekends, and it adds a small random term so that ties come out differently. Sign-up time has no effect.

Optimizer settings

Rooms and beds

Value is the quality of a bed, from 0 to 10. A queen holds one couple or one single. Couples can also take two twins in the same room. A shared living space has no gender matching.

People

Attend is the probability that the person comes on a weekend. Usual nights sets which nights they register. Flexible schedule is only used by the first-come method.
